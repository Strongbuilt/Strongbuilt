Skyline Tower

Mumbai, 2024

The Challenge

Constructing a 20-story glass facade building in a high-wind coastal zone while adhering to strict environmental green-building regulations.

The Solution

We utilized reinforced steel exoskeletons and wind-tunnel tested glass panels. A proprietary anchoring system was developed to ensure stability against cyclone-force winds.

Green Valley Villa

Lonavala, 2025

The Challenge

Integrating a luxury home into a sensitive eco-zone without disrupting local flora or altering the natural groundwater flow.

The Solution

Implemented cantilevered foundations to minimize ground contact and used sustainable bamboo architecture blended with local stone.
