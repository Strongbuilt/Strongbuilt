Common Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes, we specialize in both large-scale commercial complexes and high-end office renovations across Mumbai.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve the greater Mumbai area and surrounding districts including Thane, Navi Mumbai, and Pune for large projects.

Is the initial discussion free?

Absolutely. Reach out to us and we'll discuss your vision, budget, and timeline to see if we're the right fit — no cost, no obligation.

How long before you respond to enquiries?

Most enquiries receive a response within 1–2 working days. For urgent project timelines, call +91 96198 75081 or WhatsApp us directly.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Share the project type (residential / commercial / institutional), estimated size (sq.ft.), target location, desired start date, and your role (developer, architect, principal). The more context you provide, the faster we can route your enquiry to the right lead.
