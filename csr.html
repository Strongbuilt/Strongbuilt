Our work shapes skylines — our responsibility is to shape lives. Since 2014, we've invested in education, health, livelihoods, and the environment wherever we build.

Our Philosophy

Every Building Leaves A Legacy.
Ours Must Be Worth Living In.

A construction company's footprint extends far beyond the concrete it pours. It touches the workers who build the structure, the families who live in the neighbourhood, the ecosystems it displaces, and the generations who will inherit the skyline. Strong Built's CSR programme is our commitment to ensure that every square foot we build repays its debt to the community — in schools opened, hands trained, trees planted, and lives raised.

— Rajiv Sharma, Chairman & Managing Director

Six Focus Areas

Concentrated, measurable, outcome-driven. We'd rather do six things deeply than twenty things superficially.

Education

Rebuilding school infrastructure in under-served municipal schools, running STEM labs, and funding scholarships for meritorious students from construction-worker families.

SDG 428 Schools420 Scholars

Healthcare

Mobile health clinics, vaccination drives, maternal-care camps, and free preventive-care screenings for migrant construction workers and their families living in site colonies.

SDG 322,000+ consultations

Skill Development

NSDC-certified training in bar-bending, scaffolding, electrical, plumbing, and health-safety — with guaranteed placement in our sites or partner contractors.

SDG 83,600 Trained94% Placed

Environment

Urban reforestation, mangrove restoration along Mumbai's coastline, construction-waste recycling programmes, and water-neutrality audits across all active sites.

SDG 13 · 151.2L trees38% waste diverted

Women's Empowerment

Tailoring & textile centres, financial-literacy workshops, micro-credit partnerships, and a dedicated women-in-construction recruitment drive that has hired 340+ women to date.

SDG 57,200+ reached

Community Infrastructure

Building community halls, public toilets, drinking-water facilities, and sanitation infrastructure in tribal villages and urban slums within 10 km of our active project sites.

SDG 6 · 1148 facilities built
Deep-Impact Programmes

Our Flagships

Education · SDG 4

Foundations for Tomorrow

Since 2016, we've partnered with the BMC to refurbish municipal schools within 5km of our construction sites. Full building retrofits, modern STEM labs, libraries, safe sanitation, and solar-powered electrification. Our engineering teams donate time pro-bono to supervise every build.

28Schools Refurbished
12,400Students Reached

Skill · SDG 8

The Karigar Academy

Our Karigar Academy is an NSDC-accredited construction-trades training campus in Bhiwandi. Four-month residential programmes in RCC, formwork, plumbing, electrical, and safety — with stipends, tooling, housing, and guaranteed job placement on completion. Designed specifically for first-generation workers from rural Maharashtra.

3,600Graduates Since 2019
94%Placement Rate
₹22,000Avg. Starting Salary
Environment · SDG 13

The Sambandhan Project

A decadal mangrove restoration & urban reforestation initiative across Mumbai's salt-pan and coastal-setback lands, in partnership with Mangrove Foundation and the Maharashtra Forest Department. Every 10,000 sq.ft we build triggers the planting of 100 native saplings and 24-month maintenance oversight.

1.2 LTrees Planted
14 HaMangrove Restored
92%Survival Rate (3yr)
Health · SDG 3

The Swastha Clinic Network

Three permanent and six mobile clinics deliver primary healthcare to migrant-worker colonies and adjacent slum settlements. MBBS-led teams, specialist visiting rosters, free essential medicines, maternal & child-health camps, and TB / HIV screening partnerships with BMC's public-health network.

Voices From the Ground

"I came to the Karigar Academy from my village in Akola with nothing but a tenth-standard pass. Four months later I had a bar-bender certificate, my first toolkit, and a job on a Strong Built site in Andheri. I now earn more than my father ever did — and I'm sending my daughter to engineering college."
Ramesh Patil Karigar Academy, Batch of 2021
"Our school had broken plaster, no toilets for girls, and books from the 1990s. Strong Built didn't just write a cheque — their engineers came week after week. Today we have a STEM lab, new classrooms, and enrolment has doubled. These children finally believe school is for them."
Mrs. Savita Kulkarni Principal, BMC School No. 214
"I was trained at the women's tailoring centre in Goregaon. With the seed micro-loan, I started a uniform-stitching business. I now employ four other women from my colony. My husband used to work on construction sites; now he helps me in the shop."
Fatima Sheikh Women's Empowerment Programme, 2022

A Decade of Giving Back

2014

First Community Initiative

Strong Built is founded. The very first site in Kurla includes a small clinic for migrant workers — a seed that grows into the Swastha Network.

2016

Education Partnership Begins

First BMC school refurbishment in partnership with Pratham. A model is set: engineers-as-volunteers, donation + supervision + maintenance warranty.

2019

Karigar Academy Launched

Dedicated 3-acre training campus opens in Bhiwandi with NSDC accreditation. First batch of 60 trainees graduates with 100% placement.

2021

Sambandhan Reforestation

Mangrove restoration partnership formalised with Maharashtra Forest Department. First 40,000 saplings planted across 6 ha of degraded coastal land.

2023

₹10 Cr CSR Milestone

Annual CSR spend crosses ₹10 Cr for the first time. Independent evaluation by Sattva Consulting scores our programmes at 94/100 on outcomes.

2025

Decade Celebration

52,000+ lives impacted. Women's empowerment wing formalised. ESG Committee chaired by Independent Director Meera Kapoor begins quarterly impact reviews.
