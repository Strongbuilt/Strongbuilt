Help

Investor FAQs.

Where are the shares listed and what are the trading symbols?
Strong Built shares are listed on both the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE code: 543812) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE symbol: STRONGBLT). ISIN number is INE0LH901010. Shares can be traded through any SEBI-registered broker during market hours.
How can I transfer physical share certificates to demat?
Per SEBI regulations effective 1 April 2019, share transfers are permitted only in dematerialised form. Contact your depository participant (DP) with the share certificates, a Demat Request Form (DRF), and supporting KYC. Our RTA Link Intime India Pvt Ltd processes corporate actions; reach their investor helpdesk at +91-22-4918-6270 or rnt.helpdesk@linkintime.co.in.
What is the dividend policy?
The Board has adopted a dividend distribution policy targeting 15–20% of standalone PAT, subject to capex requirements, covenant compliance, and regulatory considerations. For FY2024–25 an interim dividend of ₹1.50 per share and a final dividend of ₹2.00 per share were declared — a payout ratio of 18.4%.
How do I update my bank mandate or nominee details?
If you hold shares in dematerialised form, update your DP directly. For physical holdings, submit Form SH-13 (nominee) or a cancelled cheque with a written request to our RTA, Link Intime India, at C-101, 247 Park, LBS Marg, Vikhroli West, Mumbai 400083.
Are unclaimed dividends eligible for IEPF transfer?
Yes — dividends unclaimed for seven consecutive years are transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) as per Section 124 of the Companies Act, 2013. Beneficiaries can claim back via Form IEPF-5 filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. A list of unclaimed dividends and the procedure is published on our website and in our annual report.
How can institutional investors schedule a 1-on-1?
Please email our Investor Relations team at investors@strongbuilt.in with your firm, assets under management, and preferred dates. We typically host 1-on-1s on the sidelines of major conferences and following each quarterly results release. Site visits to live projects can also be arranged with 2 weeks' notice.
Where can I find ESG and sustainability disclosures?
Our Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) is included in the Annual Report and follows SEBI's mandated framework. Additional ESG disclosures, including emissions intensity, safety KPIs, and CSR spend, are summarised in our CSR section and supplemented by the ESG Committee's half-yearly update.
