From Humble Roots to Skyline Icons

Strong Built traces its roots to Mumbai in 2014, starting with a single RCC project at Carter Road, Bandra. Since then we have never looked back — delivering 33+ projects, 90+ buildings, and over 5.7 million square feet of constructed area across Mumbai and beyond.

Recognised for excellence in residential, commercial, and institutional construction, we proudly partner with India's most trusted names — Rustomjee, Lodha, Mahindra Lifespaces, Hiranandani, Ajmera, Arkade, and Dheeraj Realty, among others.

Strong Built is well known for its innovation and by collaborating successfully with customers, designers, sub-contractors, consultants, and suppliers, we deliver a specialised level of service. We are continuously evolving to understand the many aspects of delivering high-value, complex construction projects with ease.
