Our Story

Forging history.

From a single project in Bandra to 33+ landmarks across Mumbai — a decade of building trust, one floor at a time.

The Journey

From humble roots to skyline icons.

Strong Built traces its roots to Mumbai in 2014, starting with a single RCC project at Carter Road, Bandra. Since then we have never looked back — delivering 33+ projects, 90+ buildings, and over 5.7 million square feet of constructed area across Mumbai and beyond.

Recognised for excellence in residential, commercial, and institutional construction, we proudly partner with India's most trusted names — Rustomjee, Lodha, Mahindra Lifespaces, Hiranandani, Ajmera, Arkade, and Dheeraj Realty, among others.

We collaborate closely with clients, designers, sub-contractors, consultants, and suppliers to deliver a specialised level of service. Continuously evolving. Understanding the many aspects of delivering high-value, complex construction projects with ease.

300+ Team Strength
A Decade of Building

The timeline.

Ten chapters. One continuous story of trust, scale, and craft.

Chapter One · Nov 2014

First Project Begins

Strong Built breaks ground on Del-sol — a 3B + P + 14F structure at Carter Road, Bandra. A modest beginning for what would become a portfolio defining Mumbai's premium residential landscape.

Del-sol · Carter Road · Bandra
Chapter Two · Dec 2015

Trust Multiplies

A second repeat order from Dheeraj signals client confidence. Ekta places the firm's first ₹100 Cr order — a defining vote of trust within the first eighteen months.

₹100 Cr Order · Repeat Mandate · Ekta · Dheeraj
Chapter Three · Dec 2016

Marquee Clientele

Within two years the firm earns the trust of Mumbai's tier-one developers — Rustomjee, Wadhwa, Hiranandani and Mahindra Lifespaces all sign on.

Rustomjee · Wadhwa · Hiranandani · Mahindra Lifespaces
Chapter Four · Dec 2017

Seven Projects Delivered

A milestone year — seven projects handed over, totalling 1.1 million square feet of completed construction across the metropolitan region.

7 Projects · 1.1M Sq.Ft Delivered

Scaling the Operation

Twenty-one live projects span the city — 2.5 million square feet under simultaneous execution. The team grows; the standard does not slip.
